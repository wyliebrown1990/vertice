## 5. The Vendr acquisition

### Takeaway
Vertice announced its acquisition of US-based Vendr on **1 June 2026**, not July; CB Insights carries a 16 June item. **Terms were not disclosed.** Vendr was a former unicorn ($1bn valuation in June 2022, about $210–216m raised) that had two rounds of layoffs in 2023–24 and a shrinking headcount. That points to a **down-round or distressed-style sale**, almost certainly well below its 2022 valuation, although the price is unconfirmed. Vertice's stated rationale is data: combining pricing benchmarks to train its AI negotiation agent, Ana. The deal also gives Vertice a large US customer base and removes its most direct "SaaS buying" competitor.

### Cited Findings
- **Date and terms:** announced 1 June 2026, terms undisclosed. Multiples.vc dates it 31 May 2026; CB Insights has a news item dated 16 June 2026 — [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/06/01/vertice-acquires-vendr-to-build-ai-powered-procurement-intelligence-platform/); [Multiples.vc](https://multiples.vc/private-comps/vendr); [CB Insights via search](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/vendr-vs-vertice)

- Strategically, the deal consolidates the two leading "SaaS buying-as-a-service" brands (Vendr in the US, Vertice in Europe). It gives Vertice US scale and arms it against Zip, Tropic, Omnea, ORO Labs and others in AI-led procurement orchestration.

### Gaps

## 6. Product scope and expansion beyond SaaS

### Takeaway
Vertice started in 2022 as a SaaS purchasing and negotiation service. It added cloud cost optimisation in 2023, then expanded in 2024–26 into intake-to-procure orchestration, embedded TPRM, document extraction, and an AI-agent layer. That layer includes **Ana**, an autonomous negotiation agent launched around Aug–Sep 2026. It now positions itself across **indirect spend** ("AI-enabled spend orchestration"), not only SaaS.

### Cited Findings
- The 2022 positioning was to "radically simplify SaaS purchasing" — [Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/london-saas-vertice-raises-24-7m/)
- **Cloud Cost Optimization** launched in 2023, alongside spend breakdowns and procurement workflows — [search summary of Vertice Series B blog](https://www.vertice.one/blog/announcing-our-series-b-funding---and-where-vertice-is-going-next)
- **Intelligent Workflows**, an intake and procurement orchestration tool, was launched in about 2024 — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vertice-launches-intelligent-workflows-a-procurement-orchestration-tool-built-to-simplify-and-accelerate-the-purchasing-process-302242576.html)
- **TPRM** is native to the buying process, with continuous monitoring, legal and compliance checks, and AI risk scoring — [Vertice sourcing page](https://www.vertice.one/platform/sourcing); [Vertice AI procurement](https://vertice.one/platform/ai-procurement-software)
- **AI agents:**
  - The Conversational Intake Agent and Compliance Agent are named, with "70+ procurement tasks" claimed — [Vertice AI procurement](https://vertice.one/platform/ai-procurement-software)
