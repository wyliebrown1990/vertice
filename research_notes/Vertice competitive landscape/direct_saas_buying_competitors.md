## Company profiles: who is growing, who is stalling, who has exited or died?

### Takeaway
From mid-2024 through Oct 2026 the category consolidated quickly. Vendr, once the $1B category leader, was absorbed by Vertice (Jun 2026). Sastrify went to Deel (May 2026). The adjacent fintechs Brex (Capital One, Apr 2026) and Airbase (Paylocity, 2024) were sold. In SaaS management, Productiv was liquidated (Jun 2026) and Torii is being sold to Freshworks (Oct 2026). Of the independents, Tropic (bookings +79% YoY, new CEO), Najar (ARR tripled) and Ramp (~$60B valuation) are growing. Spendflo, CloudEagle and Spendbase are small and thinly funded. Cledara shows signs of stalling. No independent pure-play has announced a priced equity round since Najar's Series A in Oct 2024.

## Evidence of market consolidation and pricing pressure

### Takeaway
There is clear evidence of both. Horizontal platforms are buying the pure-plays: Deel bought Sastrify, Freshworks is buying Torii, Paylocity bought Airbase and Capital One bought Brex. Ramp is bundling procurement, price benchmarks and AI negotiation briefings into a card/AP platform monetized mainly through interchange. In response, the remaining pure-plays have moved to free tiers: Vendr's free Ruth agent (Oct 2025) and Tropic's free Intelligence Hub (May 2026).

### Cited Findings
- Deel folded Sastrify into "Deel IT" as part of HR/payroll platforms moving into SaaS spend. — [UC Today](https://uctoday.com/deel-acquires-sastrify); [Deel blog](https://www.deel.com/blog/deel-acquires-sastrify/)
- Freshworks is buying Torii to extend Freshservice (ITSM) into AI and software spend governance. — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/12/3378824/0/en/freshworks-to-extend-freshservice-into-ai-and-software-spend-governance-with-agreement-to-acquire-torii.html)
- Paylocity (HCM) bought Airbase to combine payroll and non-payroll spend. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/09/04/paylocity-acquiring-corporate-spend-startup-airbase-for-325m)
- Capital One bought Brex at less than half its 2021 peak valuation. — [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/banking-law/capital-one-to-buy-fintech-brex-for-5-1-billion-in-cash-stock)
- Ramp Procurement is sold as an add-on to Ramp Plus (~$15/user/month list; $12 annual per one source), with a 60-day free trial and a promotion of up to 3 months free (to Jun 30, 2026). ### Inferences
- Buyers are platforms in adjacent categories (HR/EOR, ITSM, HCM, banks) that want the spend data and workflow, not the managed-negotiation services businesses.
- Free tiers and LLM connectors (ChatGPT/Claude) indicate that standalone benchmark lookups are being commoditized. Value is shifting to proprietary data depth, autonomous agents and workflow (intake-to-pay).
- Every pure-play is now marketing "AI agents" (Vertice "Ana," Vendr "Ruth," Spendflo "Flo AI," Ramp agents, Tropic in Claude/ChatGPT), which makes differentiation harder.

### Gaps
- No published pricing benchmarks comparing Vertice, Tropic and Spendflo contract values were found, and no evidence of price cuts by Vertice itself.
- Whether Ramp Procurement is bundled free for large customers was not confirmed. Sources say it is a paid add-on with promotions.

## Who has the largest proprietary pricing dataset?

### Takeaway
On disclosed claims, **Vertice + Vendr** now asserts the largest *negotiated-contract* pricing dataset: >$75B of spend, 250,000 contracts, 2M+ price points and 32,000 vendors. Ramp likely has the broadest *transaction* dataset ($200B annualized purchase volume, >70k customers), but much of it is card and bill-pay data, not negotiated contract line items. All figures are company claims with inconsistent definitions.

### Cited Findings
- Vertice + Vendr: >$75B of indirect spend, 250,000 negotiated contracts, 2M+ price points and 32,000 vendors (Jun 2026). Vertice bills it as the "world's largest procurement intelligence dataset." — [PR Newswire via Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/06/01/tmt-newswire/pr-newswire/vertice-acquires-vendr-to-create-the-worlds-largest-procurement-intelligence-dataset-and-lead-autonomous-ai-negotiation/2355491); [Vendr blog](https://www.vendr.com/blog/vendr-is-joining-vertice)
- Vendr alone, pre-deal: 200k+ agreements and 20k+ products (OpenPrice API). — [Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/pricing-api)
- Ramp: $200B annualized purchase volume and >70k customers (Jun 2026). Analysts say it can benchmark across 15,000+ customers' contracts (unit prices, terms). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/04/ramp-raises-750m-at-44b-valuation-as-investors-hunger-for-fintechs-with-an-ai-story/); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/c3522eb9-8437-4d1e-b266-80955ad128ac/)
- Zylo: $75B+ SaaS and cloud spend discovered and 40M+ licenses (usage and discovery data, not negotiated prices). — [Zylo](https://zylo.com/platform/alerts/)
- Tropic: >$23B spend under management (Jul 2026). — [Tropic newsroom](https://www.tropicapp.io/newsroom/tropic-grows-bookings-79-yoy-saves-customers-33m-in-h1-2026-and-deepens-its-reach-inside-claude-and-chatgpt)
- Spendflo: >$3.2B spend processed. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/spendflo-launches-flo-ai-autonomous-130000034.html)
- Sastrify (now Deel): >$2B benchmarked SaaS contracts. — [ITBrief](https://itbrief.co.nz/story/deel-acquires-sastrify-to-boost-software-spend-control)
- CloudEagle: >$2B transactions managed. — [CloudEagle](https://www.cloudeagle.ai/news/cloudeagle-ai-is-a-key-cloud-saas-management-solution-in-idc-market)

### Inferences
- Among negotiation-as-a-service specialists, Vertice+Vendr leads by a wide margin: roughly 3x Tropic's spend under management and well over 10x Spendflo, Sastrify or CloudEagle. Ramp is the main challenger to the moat claim because its data grows with card and AP volume at no marginal cost.
- The metrics are not like-for-like. "Spend covered," "spend under management," "spend processed," "spend discovered" and "contracts benchmarked" measure different things, so any "largest dataset" claim should be qualified.

### Gaps
- No independent audit or third-party comparison of dataset sizes was found.
- Ramp has not disclosed how many negotiated SaaS contracts (versus transactions) feed its benchmarks.
