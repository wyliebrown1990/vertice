## Company profiles: founding, HQ, headcount, funding, valuation, revenue, ownership, 2024–2026 news

### Takeaway
By October 2026 most first-generation independent SMPs are gone or being absorbed. **Productiv** shut down (Aug 2026). **BetterCloud** went to CoreStack (Mar 2026), **Zluri** to Infotech (Oct 2026) and **Torii** to Freshworks (deal signed Oct 2026). **Trelica** went to 1Password (Jan 2025), and **Vendr**, which had absorbed Blissfully, was bought by **Vertice** itself (Jun 2026). **Zylo** is the main scaled VC-backed pure-play still independent (~160 staff, >$72.5M raised, no new primary round since Feb 2023). **Lumos** has pivoted toward identity and agent governance.
