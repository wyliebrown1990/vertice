## Key Question 1: Company profiles (founding, HQ, headcount, funding, valuation, revenue, customers, 2024–2026 news)

### Takeaway
Zip is still the clear scale leader: a $2.2B valuation (Oct 2024, not updated since), an estimated ~$190M–$280M ARR and ~1,000 employees. It has moved into Gartner's Source-to-Pay Magic Quadrant. A second tier raised heavily in 2025–26: ORO Labs ($100M Series C, Mar 2026), Omnea ($50M B, Sep 2025), Levelpath ($55M B, Jun 2025), Pactum ($54M C, Jun 2025) and Pivot ($40M B, May 2026). A wave of agent-native seed and Series A companies also appeared (Didero and Lio, $30M each, early 2026). Tonkean has left the independent field; Coupa bought it in May 2026.

Broader "procurement software" datasets look much larger only because spend-management cards such as Ramp dominate them. By capital and scale, the leaders are Zip, then ORO Labs, then Omnea, Levelpath and Pactum, with Coupa as the incumbent consolidator.

## Key Question 3: Is the intake-to-procure category consolidating? Any acquisitions?

### Takeaway
Yes. Consolidation picked up sharply in 2025–26, led by incumbent S2P suites buying AI-native capabilities and by challengers buying data and services. The headline deals are Coupa buying Tonkean (May 2026, the first acquisition of a pure-play intake/orchestration vendor by a suite), Coupa's three other acquisitions (Cirtuo, Scoutbee, Rossum), Vertice buying Vendr (Jun 2026), Jaggaer buying Ivoflow (Sep 2026) and ORO Labs buying ProcureTech (Mar 2025).

### Cited Findings
- **Coupa → Tonkean** (announced 21 May 2026; terms undisclosed). This was Coupa's fourth recent acquisition, after **Cirtuo** (announced at Inspire 2025), **Scoutbee** (agreed late 2025) and **Rossum** (12 May 2026, at Inspire 2026). Coupa frames the four as building an "agentic trade network" and "autonomous spend management". CEO Leagh Turner called the Tonkean deal "game-changing" — [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/coupa-adds-tonkean-in-latest-ai-acquisition-push); [IT Brief](https://itbrief.co.uk/story/coupa-buys-tonkean-to-boost-autonomous-spend-management); [Procurement Magazine](https://procurementmag.com/news/coupa-acquires-tonkean-accelerating-ai-intake-systems/)
- **Vertice → Vendr** (announced 1 Jun 2026; terms undisclosed). The combined dataset covers ~$75B of indirect spend, 32,000 vendors and 250,000 negotiated contracts, and is intended to strengthen the "Ana" negotiation agent. The combined company runs 60+ procurement AI agents for 1,000+ customers (ARM, Brex, Duolingo, Twilio, Santander) [D] — [Vertice](https://www.vertice.one/blog/vertice-acquires-vendr); [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/06/01/vertice-acquires-vendr-to-build-ai-powered-procurement-intelligence-platform/)
- **Jaggaer → Ivoflow** (closed 11 Sep 2026). Ivoflow is a Koblenz-based direct-materials spend and price intelligence company founded in 2020 — [Business Wire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260913930858/en/Jaggaer-Completes-Acquisition-of-Ivoflow-Bringing-AI-Powered-Direct-Materials-Spend-Price-Intelligence-to-Manufacturers); [IT Brief](https://itbrief.co.uk/story/jaggaer-buys-ivoflow-to-boost-manufacturer-pricing)
- **ORO Labs → ProcureTech** (Mar 2025) — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250318116144/en/ORO-Labs-Acquires-ProcureTech-to-Accelerate-AI-Powered-Procurement-Orchestration)
- Earlier precedent: **Microsoft → Suplari** (Jul 2021) — [Redmond Mag](https://redmondmag.com/articles/2021/07/28/microsoft-buys-suplari.aspx); **Workday → Scout RFP** (Levelpath's founders' prior company) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/next-gen-procurement-platform-levelpath-nabs-55m/)
- Gartner's S2P research notes that adjacent intake and orchestration vendors are adding S2P capabilities, i.e. the categories are converging — [Zip-hosted Gartner MQ S2P](https://lp.ziphq.com/rs/195-QFD-187/images/eBook_Gartner_Magic_Quradrant_Source_to_Pay_Suites.pdf?version=0)

### Inferences
- Convergence is happening in both directions. Suites (Coupa, Jaggaer) are buying AI-native intake and intelligence layers, while orchestration challengers (Zip, which now sits in the S2P MQ; ORO; Omnea, which is adding SRM) are expanding into S2P. That squeezes the stand-alone "intake layer on top of Ariba/Coupa" thesis.
- With Tonkean gone, the remaining independent intake/orchestration pure-plays are Zip, ORO, Omnea, Levelpath and Pivot. Second-tier and underfunded players (Globality, Fairmarkit, Keelvar, Tropic, Order.co, Precoro, Focal Point) look like plausible acquisition targets, though no deals have been reported.
- Vertice's purchase of Vendr consolidates the SaaS-buying/negotiation niche. Its main independent rival in that niche is now Tropic, which has no reported funding since 2022.

### Gaps
- No deal values were disclosed for Tonkean, Vendr, Ivoflow, ProcureTech, Cirtuo, Scoutbee or Rossum.
- Not confirmed: the exact dates of Coupa's Cirtuo and Scoutbee deals; any 2025–26 M&A by Zip, Omnea or Levelpath (none found).
